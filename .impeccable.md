## Design Context

### Users
Someone at a desk comparing two versions of the same text — a paragraph they rewrote, a contract clause, a changelog, a message before they send it. They already have both versions; they want the marks, not a product tour.

### Brand Personality
Forensic, paper, unhurried. The interface should feel like a copy desk: quiet, exact, a little analog.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm newsprint on a wooden desk. Young Serif wordmark, Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. Vermillion for deletions (and the compare stamp), ink-blue for insertions — copyediting marks, not git green/red. Light only. Anti-references: GitHub diff chrome, dark neon dashboards, purple SaaS.

### Design Principles
- Two panes, one action. Everything else stays secondary.
- Alignment is the feature: a changed line stays on one row.
- Color is never the only signal. Strikethrough, weight, and +/− gutters travel with the highlight.
- Paper, not panels. No card stacks, no hero metrics, no decorative icons.
